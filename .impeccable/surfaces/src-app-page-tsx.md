# Home

Mode: Persuade

Audience: a business owner deciding whether to send a brief. Job: understand the offer, see shipped work, start a brief or a conversation. Proof: real projects, the published start price, MVP window, and turnkey scope. Constraint: keep forms, copy facts, and project records.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is a live interference field, and the offer sits inside it. The page refuses the glass-card studio template, the split hero with a fake product window, and a row of equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground. The only chromatic material is the supplied shader: thin RGB interference rings on black. Type is white. Display is Unbounded, body is Manrope, both already serving Cyrillic. Action color is blue #1d4ed8, used as fill on primary controls, not as a wash. Structure is hairline rules in white at low opacity. No glass, no gradient type, no orb atmosphere.

STORY: The visitor reads the offer in the field, believes the studio ships a whole product, sees work that already runs, and sends a brief or a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport shader. Fixed black bar with DAG TECH, section links, and «Обсудить проект». Centered headline «Цифровые продукты без лишнего шума», one sentence of offer, primary action «Рассчитать проект» in blue, secondary «Посмотреть решения» as an outline. A single proof line under the actions: start price, MVP window, turnkey. The next section is a legend of directions, then a ruled index of services, not cards.

FORM: User-pinned ShaderAnimation. The brief supplied the component and asked for the site to take its style, so no concept-seed roll was run. Seed key: user-pinned.

Signature interaction: the shader loop itself. Sections do not each animate in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
